# New Moon in Scorpio

**Monday Nov 9 · 12:02 am PT**

*Tropical: Scorpio · Sidereal: Libra, Vishakha · Amavasya*

## Lore

The Anglo-Saxon monk Bede, writing in the eighth century, recorded that November was once called Blōtmōnath, the month of offerings, when animals that couldn't be fed through winter were slaughtered and the rest of the herd was carried toward spring. Symbolically it is a month of honest accounting: what will you keep, and what will you let go of so the rest can last? This new moon arrives in the dark of that reckoning, a good night to count what you're carrying into winter.

## Why it matters

The new moon is the quietest point of the cycle, and November's short grey days match it. Taking stock now, without hurry, lets you enter winter deliberately, with what you need and a little less of what you don't.

## Reflection

What do you want to carry through the winter, and what has become too heavy to keep feeding?

## Small ritual

Light a candle and sit with a small piece of paper and a pen. Write one thing you're choosing to keep for the dark months, and below it one thing you're ready to stop feeding. Read both once, quietly. Fold the second and tuck it somewhere out of sight, or tear it up when you feel ready. Keep the first where you'll see it, then let the candle burn down a little before you blow it out.

## Outside

If the day allows, walk to the Puntledge River at Nymph Falls and watch for eagles along the banks and for the spawning chum in the shallows. Notice how everything there is feeding, finishing and giving itself back to the water.
